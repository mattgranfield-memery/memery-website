The problem

A mission does not make you memorable.

Your organisation means well. Your mission is solid. You have a marketing team. But meaning + mission + marketing ≠ memorable. In a media landscape dominated by doom scrolling and long-form listening, mental availability comes from having the best consumer content strategy, the deepest knowledge of individual needs, and the right channel mix to show up when it matters. If your brand can’t be found by AI, can’t earn attention in the feed, and can’t create content people genuinely choose to spend time with, you’re already falling behind.

The challenge is not simply getting attention. It is knowing who matters most, understanding what matters to them, deciding what you want each audience to remember — and then building the strategy, distinctive assets and content that make that memory more likely to form.

Problem 01

You don’t know your customer as well as you think you do.

Most organisations are making decisions from personas, old research, internal assumptions and whatever the loudest stakeholder believes. That isn’t segmentation. Until you have spoken directly to a statistically meaningful sample of your audience, identified the drivers of choice that actually separate one segment from another, and understood the pain points, barriers and motivations behind their behaviour, you are still guessing. You cannot reliably sell something, change behaviour or build mental availability around assumptions.

Problem 02

The media changed. Most agency models didn’t.

Attention has split in two directions: brutally short and deeply long. People scroll past hundreds of pieces of short-form video, then willingly spend an hour with a podcast or long-form conversation they care about. Yet much of the traditional advertising industry is still organised around campaigns, outdoor supersites, 30-second TVCs and assets cut down from them. Content for TikTok, Reels, YouTube and podcasts requires different ideas, talent, production rhythms and editorial instincts. You cannot build for a 2027 media landscape using a 2007 content model.

Problem 03

AI has rewritten the bottom of the funnel.

For years, the playbook was straightforward: build awareness, rank well on Google and make sure you appeared when someone reached the point of purchase. Generative AI has disrupted that journey. Increasingly, people are asking ChatGPT, Claude and other AI systems to compare options, explain categories and recommend who they should choose. Being visible is no longer enough. Your organisation needs to be understood, trusted and recommended by the machines increasingly shaping the shortlist.
