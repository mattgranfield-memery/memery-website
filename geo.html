A working definition

What GEO means.

It is not a magic new ranking system and there is no single AI-search algorithm. Different products use different mixtures of model knowledge, live retrieval and cited sources.

Generative Engine Optimisation is the practice of improving the chance that AI systems can find, understand, accurately represent, cite and recommend your organisation.
The framework

Five jobs for AI visibility.

We use a diagnostic sequence that follows the path from being available as a source to being confidently shortlisted.

01

Discover

Can relevant systems access the right information?

02

Understand

Is your brand, offer and expertise unambiguous?

03

Trust

Is the information distinctive, evidenced and corroborated?

04

Cite

Does the brand or its content appear as a source?

05

Recommend

Is the brand shortlisted accurately for relevant needs?

How memery can help

Start with evidence. Then fix what is actually getting in the way.

A useful GEO programme begins by establishing what AI systems currently say, where your brand appears, which sources influence the answer and where competitors are being chosen instead.

01 — Baseline

AI Visibility Audit

Build a prompt panel around your customers, category and competitors, then benchmark mentions, citations, recommendations and factual accuracy across major AI experiences.

02 — Discoverability

Technical & source audit

Check indexation, canonical pages, internal linking, crawler access, readable evidence, structured data and other fundamentals that affect whether useful material can be retrieved.

03 — Clarity

Entity & proposition clarity

Make your category, audience, geography, expertise and offer easier to understand consistently across your website, profiles, directories, partner pages and expert biographies.

04 — Evidence

Answerability & content strategy

Identify the questions customers actually ask and create useful, evidence-rich content: comparisons, constraints, case outcomes, research, expert answers and original data worth citing.

05 — Reputation

Corroboration & earned authority

Map the gap between what you claim and what credible third parties say. Strengthen the public record through genuine media, partner, customer and specialist validation — not manufactured mentions.

06 — Action

90-day GEO roadmap

Prioritise the fixes and opportunities that matter most, assign ownership, build a repeatable prompt panel and retest visibility, accuracy and qualified demand over time.

No false certainty

GEO is emerging. We separate evidence from hype.

Nobody outside the platforms knows the exact weighting behind every AI answer. So we distinguish what platform guidance confirms, what appears strategically reasonable, and what still needs to be tested.

Confirmed

Search fundamentals still matter.

For Google’s AI search features, core Search eligibility and fundamentals continue to apply.

Reasonable

Clarity and evidence should help representation.

Clear answers, distinctive evidence and credible independent support make a brand easier to understand and verify.

Test it

Do not confuse a tactic with a ranking factor.

Schema, content formatting or standalone files may be useful in context, but none should be sold as a guaranteed citation shortcut.

Measurement

A mention is not the same as a recommendation.

We measure different outcomes separately, using a fixed prompt panel as a directional benchmark alongside business signals such as referrals, branded search and qualified enquiries.

Prompt coverageHow often your brand appears across relevant prompts.
Mention shareYour mentions compared with tracked competitors.
Citation shareHow often your domains appear among tracked category citations.
Recommendation rateHow often the brand is actually shortlisted in recommendation prompts.
Accuracy rateWhether factual claims made by AI about the brand are correct.
