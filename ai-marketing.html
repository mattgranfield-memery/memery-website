What AI-native actually means

Move beyond “AI makes us faster”.

The goal is not to automate every task. It is to build a marketing system that uses AI where it improves decisions, relevance, speed or scale — while keeping human judgement visible where it matters.

01

Experimenting

Individual tools, drafting, summaries and isolated productivity gains.

02

Organising

Approved tools, basic policy, shared prompts and a clearer view of priority use cases.

03

Scaling

Documented workflows, data connections, governance, baselines and repeatable measurement.

04

AI-native

AI is embedded in how the function understands, decides, creates, personalises, discovers and operates.

Questions buyers usually ask

AI marketing FAQs.

Do we need an AI strategy if people are already using ChatGPT or Copilot?

Usually that is exactly when a playbook becomes useful. Individual experimentation can create productivity gains, but it does not automatically create shared priorities, safe data practices, repeatable workflows, measurement or organisational capability.

Will you recommend specific AI platforms?

Yes, when tool choice is part of the operating problem. But the engagement starts with the job to be done, the data available, risk and workflow — not a preferred vendor.

Can this work in an organisation with strict privacy and governance requirements?

Yes. Governance is part of the playbook, not an afterthought. We map approved tools, sensitive-data boundaries, human approval, disclosure, record keeping, IP considerations and escalation before high-risk use cases are scaled.

How technical does our marketing team need to be?

The objective is not to turn marketers into machine-learning engineers. The playbook identifies the capability your team needs, which workflows can be configured with existing tools, and where technical or data support is genuinely required.

What happens after the playbook is delivered?

The 90-day roadmap identifies pilots, owners, controls, baselines and decision points. memery can also help turn selected use cases into workflows, content systems, GEO programs, templates and operating practice.

How do we know AI is actually improving marketing?

Define the baseline first. Depending on the use case, that might mean cost and hours per asset, cycle time, incremental conversion, AI visibility, research turnaround, customer outcomes or another metric that can be tested against a control or previous process.
