Build category authority

Own a subject worth being known for. Over time, a strong series builds an association between your organisation and the ideas, problems and expertise that matter in your category.

02

Build relationships

Guests can be prospects, partners, customers and industry voices. A thoughtful interview creates a useful reason to start or deepen a relationship — without beginning with a sales pitch.

03

Build a content engine

The episode is only the beginning. One recording can feed YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram, articles, newsletters, search, thought leadership and sales conversations.
