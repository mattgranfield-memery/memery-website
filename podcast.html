Why podcasting?

Attention is useful.
Memory is better.

A good podcast gives brands something most marketing formats struggle to create: sustained attention. Done properly, it can build familiarity, authority and trust over time — while creating a growing library of content your sales and marketing teams can keep using.

The opportunity

Your audience will give you 30 minutes. If you earn it.

People do not voluntarily spend half an hour listening to an advertisement. They will spend it on something useful, surprising, entertaining or genuinely interesting. The job is to find the intersection between what your audience cares about, what your organisation has credibility talking about, and what supports your strategy.

The mistake

Don't make a corporate podcast.

Make a good podcast that happens to be made by a corporation. If the idea starts with your products, awards and executives, the audience can feel it. Start with the listener instead.

01

Build category authority

Own a subject worth being known for. Over time, a strong series builds an association between your organisation and the ideas, problems and expertise that matter in your category.

02

Build relationships

Guests can be prospects, partners, customers and industry voices. A thoughtful interview creates a useful reason to start or deepen a relationship — without beginning with a sales pitch.

03

Build a content engine

The episode is only the beginning. One recording can feed YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram, articles, newsletters, search, thought leadership and sales conversations.
